To protect you and us, this page explains privacy, legal boundaries in Germany, and who is responsible for what. It’s short. Promise.

Data privacy (what we do with your data)

  • Purpose. Conversations are recorded locally to transcribe speech into text. Raw voice is not published by default.
  • Transient processing. Data is processed transiently for the session to generate anonymized output (speech-to-text; profanity replaced with neutral tokens; optional text-to-speech for publication).
  • Publication only with consent. Only anonymized text/synthetic audio and avatar video may be shared publicly, if you consent.
  • Minimal storage. We keep a consent record and anonymized materials. You may request access or deletion via your consent code.
  • Local device memory. This page stores a small “consent confirmed” flag in your browser’s localStorage so you’re not asked again.

Free speech, within German law

We value open inquiry. However, participation is subject to German law. In particular, you must not post or advocate content prohibited under German criminal law, including Volksverhetzung (incitement to hatred, e.g. §130 StGB), threats of violence, defamation, or unlawful harassment. If you are unsure, stay on the safe, respectful side.

Liability & platform stance

  • Your views are yours. All statements and opinions expressed by participants are solely those of the individual speaker.
  • No endorsement. The platform owner and organizers do not endorse, adopt, or verify participant statements.
  • Reservation of rights. We may decline to publish, edit, or remove content that appears unlawful or violates our ground rules.
